Frequently asked questions

Clear answers for your accommodation search, from your first shortlist to the day you move in.

i

Take your time.
Never feel pressured to pay a deposit before you have checked the property and the person offering it.

How do I find accommodation near my university?

Start with your university and the area where you would prefer to stay. Compare available boarding houses and shared accommodation by location, price, facilities and house rules. Contact the provider to confirm that the space is still available and ask how far it is from campus.

What does a “squatting space” or shared space mean?

People may use these terms to describe sharing a room or living space with another person. The exact arrangement can differ, so ask how many people will share the room, what facilities are shared, what the total cost is and what privacy you can expect before agreeing.

What should I check before paying a deposit?

Where possible, visit the property in person or ask someone you trust to visit. Confirm the provider’s identity, the address, rent and deposit amounts, utility costs, house rules, move-in date and refund conditions. Get the agreement and proof of payment in writing. Be cautious of pressure to pay immediately.

Are all accommodation listings guaranteed or verified by Cohably?

Do not assume a listing has been independently verified unless Cohably clearly says so on that listing. Availability, prices and conditions can change. Confirm important details directly with the accommodation provider and check the property before making a payment.

How can I tell whether a place is right for my budget?

Look beyond the advertised rent. Ask about deposits, bills, water, electricity, internet, transport, cleaning costs and any other fees. Work out the full monthly cost and leave some room for food, study supplies and unexpected expenses.

What should I do if information in a listing seems suspicious?

Pause the conversation and do not send money or sensitive documents. Keep the listing link and relevant messages, then use any reporting option available on the site or contact Cohably through its published support channel. If you have already lost money, consider contacting your bank or mobile-money provider and the relevant authorities.

Does Cohably arrange or guarantee my accommodation?

Cohably is intended to help learners discover accommodation options. Unless a specific service explicitly says otherwise, you should confirm availability, agree on terms and make arrangements directly with the provider. Read all details carefully before committing.
