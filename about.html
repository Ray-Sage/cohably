WHY COHABLY EXISTS01 / OUR PURPOSE

A simpler search.
A more confident start.

For many new learners, choosing a university is only one part of the journey. There is also the question of where to live, how much it will cost and whether the place will suit everyday student life.

Cohably is being built to make that search easier to navigate. We bring the accommodation search closer to the student’s university decision, so learners can explore options, compare the details that matter and ask informed questions before they commit.

OUR MISSION

Help learners spend less energy searching—and more energy preparing for what comes next.

Student life begins before the first lecture. A clear place to start can make that transition feel more manageable.

THE COHABLY APPROACH

From search to a smarter decision.

Use a few practical steps to make your accommodation search more focused.

01

Choose your university

Start with the institution you plan to attend and the area where you would like to live.

02

Compare your options

Look at location, total cost, facilities, room-sharing arrangements and house rules.

03

Confirm before committing

Ask questions, check the property and agree on costs and conditions before paying.

✳ Cohably helps you explore options. Always confirm current details with the accommodation provider.

A NOTE ON TRUST

Explore carefully. Decide confidently.

Accommodation details can change, and a listing should not be treated as a guarantee unless the site explicitly says so. Confirm availability, the provider’s identity, full costs and house rules directly. Whenever possible, inspect the property before making a payment.
